ABOUT

Gokulakrishnan A B is the Founder & Director of Dark Pixels, a technology company building enterprise software, AI products and digital platforms for growing businesses. He also serves as Chief Technology Officer of VtoV Superfresh and Chief Executive Officer of DarkPixels AI.

His work bridges strategy and execution — shaping product vision, architecting systems built to scale, and leading delivery from concept to production. Under his leadership, Dark Pixels has delivered 35+ client platforms, deployed full-scale ERP and CRM systems that power day-to-day business operations, and is developing an EDII-funded, AI-powered autonomous drone platform for real-time greenhouse gas monitoring.
